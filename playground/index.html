Dive in and try Pelagic yourself.

Every card below is a small challenge for one Pelagic feature: decode a message with Chat, let the Agent fill out a form, run commands with no AI at all, and find what DevTools can see. Solve one and it turns into a pearl.

  1. Open the AI panel with the sparkle button or Ctrl+Shift+A.
  2. Pick the mode on the card (Chat, Agent, Compare or Local) and paste its prompt. Every prompt has a Copy button.
  3. Type what Pelagic tells you into the answer box, or watch the card light up on its own.

    About CAPTCHAs

    Pelagic's Agent never tries to solve or get around a CAPTCHA or "verify you're human" check. When one shows up it stops, asks you to solve it in the page, and carries on when you press Send. Those checks exist to tell people from bots, so this playground doesn't include one to crack.
